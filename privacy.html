MPlayer – Privacy Policy

Effective 27 September 2026 · App: MPlayer (com.mplayer.app) · Developer: KM Soft Store

MPlayer is a music and video player. It is built to work on your phone without collecting anything about you. This page explains exactly what the app uses and why.

Information we collect

None. MPlayer has no account or sign-in, no analytics and no advertising. The developer does not receive any personal information, usage data, files or recordings from the app.

What stays on your phone

  • Your playlist, the song you were listening to and where you stopped, and your settings (language, look, repeat, shuffle, speed, dancer, equaliser). These are kept in the app's own storage so it can carry on where you left off, and are deleted when you uninstall the app.
  • Files you make with the Tools (a cut part of a song or video, or sound taken out of a video) are saved on your phone only.

Permissions

  • Microphone (record audio). Used only so the sound bars and the dancer can follow the song MPlayer is playing. Android treats listening to an app's own sound output as using the microphone. MPlayer does not record your voice or your surroundings, does not save any audio, and sends nothing anywhere.
  • Music and videos / storage. Used to open the songs, videos and folders you choose.
  • Notifications and foreground service. Used to keep playing when the app is in the background and to show the player controls in the notification and on the lock screen.
  • Internet. Used only by the web tab, which opens video websites you choose.

Websites opened in the app

The web tab shows third-party websites the same way a browser does. When you visit them, those websites may collect information and set cookies under their own privacy policies, which MPlayer does not control. Their cookies are kept on your phone so you stay signed in, and are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Sharing

MPlayer does not sell, share or transfer any data, because it does not collect any.

Children

MPlayer does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
